NOTES FOR DEVELOPERS
- Logo SVGs are traced from the original artwork (98.7% match). Text in the
  logo/print SVGs is converted to outlines, so they look the same everywhere.
- The return-policy SVGs keep the Arabic as live text with the font embedded;
  they display correctly in browsers. Figma/Illustrator may swap the font —
  use the PNG there.
- QR codes open https://www.instagram.com/nuriya.eg (all tested). Keep the
  white margin around the code; print no smaller than 2 x 2 cm.
- Photos are PNG only: a photo cannot become a real vector, and an "SVG" of a
  photo is just the same pixels inside an SVG wrapper.
- Original uploads are kept at their original resolution; upscaling them
  would not add real detail.
