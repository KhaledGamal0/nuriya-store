Our story

From Nur, نور. Light.

Nuriya started in Cairo as a dream: one soft, oversized piece a girl reaches for first. Modest by design, with long sleeves, a loose cut and a longer curved hem. It does the talking through fabric, embroidery and color, never logos or noise.
