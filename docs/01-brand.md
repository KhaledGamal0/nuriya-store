# 01 — Nuriya brand bible (condensed from brand book, Oct 7 2026)

Full source: `brand-assets/reference/Nuriya-Brand-Book.pdf` (17 pages).

## Identity
- **Name:** Nuriya — from "Nur" (نور) = light
- **What:** Cairo women's clothing — comfy, oversized everyday pieces with a quiet, old-money feel
- **Tagline:** comfy · everyday pieces  **Hero line:** Not loud. Just unforgettable.
- **Instagram:** @nuriya.eg (verified) — today: DM to order, courier delivery with inspection on receipt
- **Audience:** young women — university students & early-career girls. Modest & hijab-friendly by design (long sleeves, loose cut, longer curved hem). One piece styles both ways: old-money (pleated trousers, belt, leather bag) and casual (jeans/leggings, sneakers, cap)
- **Positioning:** quiet luxury at an everyday price. Fabric, embroidery, color do the talking — not logos/noise. Audience poll Old money vs Casual: **57% / 43%** → lead hero content with old money
- **Mission:** the everyday piece a girl reaches for first — comfy enough for a lecture, polished enough for coffee after
- **Values:** quiet confidence · comfort first · craft in the details · made with love (a dream turned into a brand) · versatile
- **Personality:** soft, warm, elegant, a little nostalgic (autumn, coffee, croissants, journals). Feminine without being girly-pink loud

## Logo
- Handwritten script wordmark **Nuriya** — ALWAYS the original artwork file (SVG in `kit/01_Logo/svg/`). **Never retype in a font** (AI has produced "Nuliya", "Nautija")
- Lockups: wordmark alone (default, website header) · wordmark + line + tagline (hero, packaging, size guide) · wordmark on pink circle (IG avatar) · small embroidered script (garment)
- Colors: Plum on Blush pink (primary) · Pink/white on plum or burgundy (reverse) · white or plum on photos · red-burgundy on white woven neck label
- Clear space ≥ height of "N". Min size 120 px wide on screen / 25 mm print. Centre or left align. Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, gradient, busy backgrounds

## Typography
- Logo: script artwork only
- Display headlines: tall condensed high-contrast serif, ALL CAPS, slightly tight (the "STYLING INSPO" Canva font — exact name OPEN; web stand-in Bodoni Moda / Playfair Display)
- Section titles: Poppins 500, caps, letter-spacing 0.2em
- Body/UI: Poppins 400, sentence case
- Tagline: Poppins 400 lowercase, letter-spaced, rose gold
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic (RTL layout, not just translated text)
- Scale (desktop/mobile): H1 56/36 · H2 32/26 · H3 caps 14/13 · body 16/15 lh 1.6 · small 13
- Max 2 fonts + logo per design

## Voice
Stylish best friend: short, warm, confident, never pushy. English first, Arabic where it helps (policy, service).
Lines that work: "Not loud. Just unforgettable." · "For the quiet confidence girl ✨" · "Four fits, one vibe. Tell me which one's yours?" · "Every stitch, every detail — handcrafted quality" · "Something beautiful is on its way." · "Ur a vibe girl" · "Thank you for supporting a dream that started with love." · "Get yours now / DM to order"
Use: comfy, everyday, effortless, quiet confidence, soft, oversized, crafted, details, vibe, styling.
Avoid: cheap, sale-sale-sale, hype, "best quality in Egypt", >2 emojis.

## Product — QUIET CONFIDENCE quarter-zip (hero, only product now)
- Price: **1200 EGP** both colorways
- Colorways (same design reversed): **Cream** (cream body, burgundy collar + embroidery, cream tonal placket) · **Burgundy** (burgundy body, pure-white collar, cream/white embroidery, burgundy tonal placket)
- Both: silver zip teeth + round ring pull
- Embroidery: chest "QUIET CONFIDENCE" serif caps, wide spacing, raised satin stitch, slight upward arc, below placket end · small "Nuriya" script above right end (over "DENCE") · left sleeve near cuff: "do what you love / love what you do" script, rising
- Construction: pointed polo/rugby contrast collar, quarter-zip, oversized body, dropped shoulders, long sleeves, ribbed cuffs, curved rounded hem, soft sweatshirt fabric (composition OPEN), woven neck label (white tape, red-burgundy script)

## Photography
Editorial & calm: off-white backgrounds, natural light, burgundy piece as the one strong color. Avoid dim light (burgundy → black). Always one clear embroidery frame. Props in palette (browns, creams, black, denim, gold jewelry). Models natural, smiling, modest.
Sizes: product 2000×2500 (4:5) · hero desktop 2400×1200 (2:1) · IG 1080×1350 · story 1080×1920.
Content formats: Styling Inspo board (binder rings, Pantone chips) · Uni Fits Inspo (paper stack + paperclip) · Palette of the Season · This-or-That poll · Autumn Essentials flat-lay · Model shots · Detail reels · Pink hang tag "coming soon".
Performance insight (IG screens): real-girl reels 19.5K / 4.7K / 2.9K / 1.7K / 1.5K views vs styling boards 216–523 → **real people first**, boards as styling layer.

## Packaging

## Website rules from the brand book
Feels like the Instagram: soft pink & white space, big calm photos, plum type, almost no decoration. Mobile first (traffic from IG). One message per section. No stock photos. Logo as SVG. Arabic = real RTL.
Components: header (logo centred, menu left, bag right, pink, hairline) · primary button plum fill/pink text, pill, Poppins 500 caps spaced · secondary plum outline · product card white/tint, 18 px radius, no/very soft shadow · section title = rose-gold caps label above plum serif headline · tables plum header/pink text, tint alternate rows · footer plum bg, pink text, script logo, tagline, IG link.
